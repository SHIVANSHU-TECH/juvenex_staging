## 📞 Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1: Cold Outreach (Week 1-4)

#### 1.1 Direct Calling Script

> **Opening:**
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with GLP Guide. We're working with [number] clinics across the country to help them manage their GLP-1 patients more effectively."

> **Value Prop:**
"Do you currently have a way for your patients to track their meals, glucose, and stay connected between visits - without you or your staff manually following up?"

> **If they say yes:**
"That's great. What does that look like today? How is it working for you?"

> **If they say no:**
"That's actually what we're solving for clinics like yours. We built a white-label app that lets patients track everything, get AI-generated meal plans, and stay connected - and the clinic can message all their patients instantly."

> **Close:**
"Can I send you over some information? I'd love to show you how it works."

## 📈 Scripts & Objections

### Common Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have something" | "That's great. I'd love to see what you're using. Honestly, we might not be a fit - but I can show you what's working for other clinics so you can compare." |
| "Too expensive" | "I hear that. Let me ask - what's your budget for patient retention tools? Actually, most clinics find that we pay for ourselves in the first 60 days through reduced staff time alone." |
| "I'm not interested" | "Totally understand. Can I just send you some information? No follow-up required. That way you have it for the future." |
| "Send me info" | "Absolutely. I'll send over a brief overview. My direct number is [phone] if you have any questions after you review it." |

### Closing Lines

> "Does this make sense so far? Great. What's the next step to get you set up?"

> "Would it make sense to get your clinical director involved in the demo? They might have some questions I can answer."

> "I can have you fully set up within 24 hours once we move forward. What works best for you - Thursday or Friday to launch?"
